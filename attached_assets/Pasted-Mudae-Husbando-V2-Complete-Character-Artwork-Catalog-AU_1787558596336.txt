5. SOURCE QUALITY

Use stable, appropriate image URLs wherever possible.

Prefer artwork that is:

clearly the correct character
visually suitable for a Discord character card
reasonably high quality
accessible directly by Discord
unlikely to immediately break
not obviously watermarked
not misleading

Avoid:

random search-result URLs
temporary URLs
local filesystem paths
URLs requiring authentication
URLs that only work in a browser session
tracking URLs
questionable third-party uploads when a better source is available

Do not build a web scraper.

Do not introduce an automated image-scraping pipeline.

This is a curated catalog, not an image crawler.
